This page is for connecting the desktop app and finding your way around it.

The app comes with fleetd on any computer with a display, and updates itself. Shortcuts below use ⌘; on Linux, use
Ctrl.

## Connect it {#pair}

When the app first opens, pick one:

- **Sign in**: for a fleetd cloud workspace.
- **Use your own workspace**: for a workspace you run yourself.

On the workspace's own computer, the app connects by itself. With no workspace yet, click **Set up fleetd on this Mac** (on Linux,
**this computer**) to run the setup in a terminal.

To connect from another computer:

1. On the workspace, run `fleetd pair`. It prints a pairing code and the workspace's address.
2. In the app, under **Or connect to a workspace on another computer**, enter the address and the code.
3. Click **Connect**.

The code works once, for 15 minutes. To pair a device that may only watch, use `fleetd pair --role read`; to let it
drive agents but not change the workspace's settings, `--role operate`.

To add another workspace, open **Settings › Workspaces & devices** and click **Add a workspace**.

## The window {#window}

- **Sidebar** (left): your bots, newest first. A dot means a bot needs you. Right-click a bot to pin it, archive it, or
  move it to a section. At the bottom: your picture (Settings, Appearance, Help), **Connections**, **Screens** and
  **Factory**.
- **Chat** (middle): your conversation with the bot's lead. See [Chat with the lead](/docs/chat).
- **Panel** (right): the bot's agents and work, in tabs. Open it with the agents button at the top right, or ⌥⌘B.

Drag a sidebar or panel edge to resize it; double-click the edge to reset it.

To make a bot, click **+** at the top of the sidebar, or drop a folder or GitHub link on the window.

## The panel {#panel}

### Agents {#agents}

The bot's agents at work, those waiting on you first. Click one to watch what it's doing: its commands, edits and
messages. Watching never interrupts it. Under them, **Screens** lists the [screens](/docs/browser-sessions) they use.

### DMs between agents {#dms}

Agents message each other directly: to share a finding, ask about a file, or answer a question. Their messages show
as cards in each agent's work. To read them all, open **History** and filter to **DMs**.

### Shipping {#train}

The bot's pull requests: those that need a look first, then what the [merge train](/docs/merge-train) merges now, then
the ones in line. Click one to open it.

### Plan {#checklist}

The lead's to-do list for the bot. You can delete items or **Clear** done ones; to add or change one, tell the lead.

### History {#activity}

What happened while you were away: agents starting and finishing, reviews, merges, and what you answered.
**Decisions**, at its top, lists what you and the lead have decided.

### Artifacts {#artifacts}

Reports and files shared in the chat. The tab shows once there are some.

## When the workspace is out of reach {#offline}

Your bots and recent chats stay on screen, marked **Offline**. You can keep writing: messages send by themselves once
the app reconnects. Approvals, secrets and Stop wait until the workspace is back.

## Where time went {#time}

Press ⌘; or click **Factory** in the sidebar, then **Agent time**: a timeline of where a piece of work's time went.
See [Where time went](/docs/agent-time).

## Settings {#settings}

Press ⌘, to open Settings. Type in **Search settings** to find any option. Settings has three groups:

- **You**: General (name, appearance, notifications), Accounts (your Claude and GitHub), Vault, Your memory, Workspaces &
  devices.
- **Bots**: each bot's settings: repos, agents, instructions, automations, vault variables.
- **Workspace** (owners and admins only): People, Machines, Providers, Bot defaults, Setup, and Billing on a cloud workspace.

What changes a teammate's work outside your bot is in Workspace.

### Workspace {#workspace}

- **People**: who's on the workspace, their roles, and invites.
- **Machines**: the machines, what runs where, and updates. See [Machines](/docs/machines#page).
- **Providers**: what agents run on (Anthropic, Google), how each is connected, and the profiles under each.
- **Bot defaults**: the default profile, the lead's profile, skills and instructions for every bot.
- **Setup**: what the workspace is connected to and the steps still left.

## Shortcuts {#shortcuts}

⌘/ lists every shortcut. ⌘K finds any command or setting by name.

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| ⌘K | Command palette |
| ⌘L | Focus the message box |
| ⌘F | Find in the chat |
| ⌘B | Show or hide the sidebar |
| ⌥⌘B | Show or hide the panel |
| ⌘1 to ⌘9 | Open that bot |
| ⌘[ / ⌘] | Previous or next bot |
| ⌘. | Next bot that needs you |
| ⇧⌘[ / ⇧⌘] | Previous or next workspace |
| ⌘; | Factory |
| ⌘, | Settings |
| ⇧⌘A | Attach a file |
| ⇧⌘↩ | Hand a screen back to its agent |
| Esc | Close a menu, panel or sheet |
