You'll pair the desktop app with your [hub](/docs/glossary#hub) and use it to chat with each project's
[lead](/docs/glossary#lead).

The app is installed with fleetd on any machine with a display. To add it on Linux without a display, see
[Install](/docs/install#options). It updates itself; `fleetd update` updates it too. Shortcuts below use ⌘; on Linux,
use Ctrl.

## Pair it with the hub {#pair}

The first time it opens, the app says what fleetd is and shows three steps that tick themselves as you go: connect to
your hub, add a project, say hi to its lead.

With no hub on this computer yet, click "Set up fleetd on this Mac": it opens `fleetd init` in Terminal (on Linux, your
terminal). When setup finishes, come back to the app and it pairs by itself. If no terminal opens, the app shows the
command to copy. On the hub machine, the app pairs by itself
when it opens. On another computer:

1. On the hub, print a pairing code:

   ```console capture=pair
   $ fleetd pair
   Pairing code: 38X0-57P8  (single use, valid until 2026-10-18T22:43:43.690Z)
   Hub address:  http://127.0.0.1:4778
   Pairing link: fleetd://pair?hub=http%3A%2F%2F127.0.0.1%3A4778&code=38X0-57P8

   The fleetd app on this machine pairs by itself when it opens. On another device, open the
   app and enter the code and hub address above, or open the link there.
   ```

2. In the app, under "Or connect to a hub on another computer", enter the hub address and the code. Or open the
   `fleetd://pair` link.

The code works once, for 15 minutes. Capitals and the dash are optional: `abcd2345` works as `ABCD-2345`. To connect with a key instead, click "Use a key instead".

To change hubs, open Settings (⌘,) → General → Hub.

## The window {#window}

| Part | Shows |
|---|---|
| Sidebar | Your projects, each saying why it wants you: "reply", "approve", "secret" or "blocked". Otherwise it says how many agents are working. A project in bold has a new message from its lead. Below them: Agents, Status, Time, Machines and Settings. |
| The chat | The selected project's conversation with its lead. |

Click a project to open its chat. ⌥⌘A jumps to the next project that wants you. To add a project, click "Add" next to Projects, then paste a GitHub URL or
`owner/repo`. You can also drop a folder or a GitHub link anywhere on the window, a chat included. Other files dropped
on a chat attach to your message. See [Add a project](/docs/projects#add-a-project).

## The chat {#chat}

| Part | Does |
|---|---|
| Messages | The conversation, with Markdown, attachments and pasted images. Older history loads as you scroll up, including work from before the chat existed. |
| The box at the bottom | Your message ("Message the lead"), ready to type as soon as a chat opens. Enter sends; Shift+Enter adds a line; ↑ in an empty box brings back your last message; the clip or ⌘⇧A attaches a file. While the lead works, a message steers it, and Stop beside Send ends its turn now. Unsent words stay, even after you quit. |
| The line above the box | While the lead waits on you, what for ("2 questions, asked at 09:02", "1 command to approve"). Show scrolls to it. |
| An empty chat | Asks what the lead should do, with a few starters that fill the box for you to edit. |
| The agents strip | Under the lead's message, after any approval or secret it asks for: how many agents are working, and on which PRs. Expand it to see each agent, and click one to read what it was told. "Where the time went" opens [Time](#time), with each agent's model, tokens and activity. |
| One-line events | Pull requests opened, merged or sent back. |
| Secret and approval items | A masked field with "Save secret", or a command (or a tool's permission request) with Approve and Deny. See [Secrets & approvals](/docs/approvals-and-secrets). |
| The header | The project, its state word (as in [Status](#status)), and its [Max agents](/docs/projects#max-agents) control ("agents 6 / 12"). |

### When the hub is out of reach {#offline}

Your projects and recent chats stay on screen, marked "Offline, as of 09:02". You can keep writing: each message shows
"queued" and sends by itself when the app reconnects, once, even if you quit in between. Edit or Dismiss takes a queued
message back. The banner says why the hub doesn't answer and when the app tries again; Retry now tries at once.
Approvals, secrets and Stop wait until the hub is back.

Once you're connected again, a chat says "Last known" until its newest messages load; if they can't, it says so, with
Retry. "Disconnect and forget key" in Settings clears what the app kept for that hub, queued messages included.

How to work with the lead: [Chat with the lead](/docs/chat).

## Agents {#agents}

Agents lists every agent working now, across all your projects: its role, its task, what it's doing and for how long.
Click one to watch its work as it happens: what it was told, the commands it ran, the files it edited and what it's
writing. Watching doesn't interrupt it. To change what it does, tell its lead in the chat.

## Status {#status}

![Status with one row per project: billing waiting on you at 64%, checkout blocked at 31%, storefront working at 78% with five agents, each with its last merge.](/docs/img/status.png)

Status shows one row per project: a state word (waiting on you, blocked, working or idle), its two-tone progress bar,
the agents running, and when the last pull request merged. Click a row to open that project's chat, or PRs for its
pull requests; hover it for detail.
See [Status](/docs/status).

## Where time went {#time}

Open Time from the sidebar, the agents strip in a chat, or the command palette. It has three tabs:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| Timeline | One piece of work: a lane per agent, PR and CI check, and the waits on you, with the [critical path](/docs/glossary#critical-path) marked. Each agent's lane shows its model and output tokens. Hover a bar for the command, its duration and exit code; a transcript's "Open the chat" goes to the project's chat. |
| Breakdown | Agent time by group, with the slowest commands and CI checks. |
| Insights | Findings, each with the spans behind it. |

![Where a piece of work's time went: a lane per agent, PR and CI check, the waits on you, the critical path underlined, and the legend.](/docs/img/time.png)

Times are in your own time zone. How to read it: [Where time went](/docs/time-inspector).

## Settings {#settings}

Open Settings with ⌘,. It takes the sidebar's place; Back returns to where you were. Type in "Search settings" to find
an option or a prompt by name, and press Enter to open the first match.

| Section | Has |
|---|---|
| General | Appearance (light, dark, match system), motion, the hub connection, whether `fleetd://` pairing links open in this app, Claude auto memory, and how long time data is kept. |
| Agents | [Max agents](/docs/projects#max-agents) for each project, and the cap for the whole fleet. Each slider applies when you let go, with Undo. |
| Instructions | Your own text, added to every agent's first message. |
| Prompts | The prompts fleetd sends agents, listed under Prompts in the sidebar, to read and edit, and any of your edits that no longer apply. See [What agents are told](/docs/what-agents-are-told). |

## Shortcuts {#shortcuts}

⌘/ shows every shortcut in the app, and ⌘K finds any command by name. ⌘ is Ctrl on Linux.

The command palette (⌘K) opens on the project you're in. Type a setting's own name ("max agents", "dark") to find it. What you open most rises to the top. Press ⌘K again to see the other actions for the selected item, such as a project's instructions.

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| ⌘K | Command palette |
| ⌘/ | Keyboard shortcuts |
| ⌘L | Focus the message box |
| ⌘B | Toggle the sidebar |
| ⌘1 to ⌘9 | Open that project in the sidebar |
| ⌘[ / ⌘] | Previous / next project |
| ⌥⌘A | Next project that wants you |
| ⌘. | Status |
| ⌘, | Settings |
| ⌘⇧A | Attach a file |
| Enter / Shift+Enter | Send the message / new line |
| ↑ (empty box) | Bring back your last message |
| Esc | Close the menu, panel or sheet |
