    Using fleetd

    The desktop app

    The app is where you give work, follow it, answer asks, approve commands and enter secrets. Install it with curl -fsSL https://fleetd.sh | sh -s -- --app (Install). It updates itself; fleetd update updates it too.

    Pair it with the hub#

    On the hub machine the app pairs by itself. On another computer, print a code on the hub:

    $ fleetd pair
Pairing code: 38X0-57P8  (single use, valid until 2026-10-18T22:43:43.690Z)
Hub address:  http://127.0.0.1:4778
Pairing link: fleetd://pair?hub=http%3A%2F%2F127.0.0.1%3A4778&code=38X0-57P8

The fleetd app on this machine pairs by itself when it opens. On another device, open the
app and enter the code and hub address above, or open the link there.

    Enter the address and code in the app, or open the fleetd://pair link. The code works once, for 15 minutes. The app keeps its key encrypted with the OS keychain; without one (Linux with no keyring, an unsigned Mac build) it keeps it in a file only you can read. Settings (⌘,) shows the hub it is paired with and disconnects it; "Connect to a hub…" in the palette pairs with another.

    Give work#

    The app opens on the board, ready to type: say what the fleet should do and press Enter. The board shows what needs you and every open order with its agents, PRs and how far along the merge train each is.

    • Project: the project picker (⌘⇧P) or a leading @project . Without one, the hub picks and says why.
    • Type: the type picker (⌘⇧T), or a prefix: fix: or bug:, research:, finish:. The default is a feature.
    • Attach: up to 8 files (images, text, PDF; 5 MiB each) with ⌘⇧A, paste or drop. A paste over 3,500 bytes becomes pasted-N.txt. The lead finds them in .fleetd/attachments/<W>/ of the project, kept out of git.
    • Mention: @ offers the project's files, orders and PRs.

    An order is at most 4,000 bytes; attach the detail. If the hub can't tell the project, the order asks which one. To add a project, use Add next to Projects: paste a GitHub URL or owner/repo, or drop a folder or a link on the app (Add a project).

    Follow an order#

    Open an order for its agents, PRs and done-when checks, and the project lead's conversation about it. The box under the conversation talks to the lead:

    DoHowThe lead
    Add to the lead's queueEntertakes it in at its next step, after the tool it is running (what that means)
    Interrupt and redirect⌘Enterstops its current step and reads it at once
    Stop⌘⇧., then confirmits running agents are cancelled and its asks withdrawn; merged PRs stay
    ReorderReorder, on a stopped orderits text goes back into the composer, to send as a new order

    Each steer shows whether it was queued, delivered and acknowledged (Receipts); a queued one can be withdrawn. Pick an agent to steer that agent instead. A stopped order isn't reopened.

    Status#

    ⌘. shows Status for what you're looking at: the open order, the selected project, or the whole fleet. It shows the progress fleetd verified, what the work waits on, what changed since you last looked, health, what needs you and risks. It stays live; a part whose source went quiet is marked stale with the reason and keeps its last value. What the number means: Status.

    Inbox and replies#

    ⌘I opens the inbox. 1-9 select an item, Enter takes its recommendation, n answers no. Or answer a whole batch in one line in its reply field (or in the palette):

    ReplyMeans
    1 goaccept item 1 (also yes, y, ok, rec)
    2 nvmreject item 2 (also no, drop, n)
    3 bpick option b of item 3 (a to f; a is the recommendation)
    4 skipskip a question for now: the lead keeps its recommendation, nothing is recorded
    1-3 gothe same answer for items 1 to 3
    2: use the staging bucketanswer in your own words
    4 laternot now; it stays open
    5 doneyou did a checklist's steps
    recs fineaccept every recommendation not mentioned
    #B4 1 goanswer batch 4, not the latest
    pref: no new dependenciesa standing preference for every lead
    forget D12drop decision 12

    Mix them: 1 go 2 nvm 3 b. A reply the app can't read changes nothing. You have 10 seconds to take back an answer to a question before its lead hears of it. From a terminal, fleetd answer --key-file <human key> lists the open questions, and fleetd answer "1 b" --key-file … replies.

    Asks that block work also raise a desktop notification: at most one every 20 minutes, with the rest folded in, and only urgent ones in quiet hours (23:00 to 08:00 unless a policy.quiet_hours decision says otherwise).

    Where time went#

    Time, in an order's header, the sidebar or the palette, shows where the time of an order, a project, a machine or the fleet went: each agent, PR, CI check and ask on a timeline with the critical path marked, totals by group with the slowest commands and checks, and the findings. Open a step to read its transcript. How to read it, and what is not exact: Where time went.

    Settings#

    Settings (⌘,) has General (appearance, motion, the hub connection and the hub's own options), Prompts and Instructions; the last two are in What agents are told.
