    Using fleetd

    The desktop app

    The app is where you give work, follow it, answer asks, approve commands and enter secrets. Install it with curl -fsSL https://fleetd.sh | sh -s -- --app (Install).

    Pair it with the hub#

    On the hub machine the app pairs by itself. On another computer, print a code on the hub:

    $ fleetd pair
Pairing code: 38X0-57P8  (single use, valid until 2026-10-18T22:43:43.690Z)
Hub address:  http://127.0.0.1:4778
Pairing link: fleetd://pair?hub=http%3A%2F%2F127.0.0.1%3A4778&code=38X0-57P8

The fleetd app on this machine pairs by itself when it opens. On another device, open the
app and enter the code and hub address above, or open the link there.

    Enter the address and code on the app's "Connect to your fleetd hub" screen, or open the fleetd://pair link. The code works once, for 15 minutes. The app keeps its key in the OS keychain (an unsigned Mac build: in a file only you can read). Settings (⌘,) shows the hub it is paired with and disconnects it; "Connect to a hub…" in the palette pairs with another.

    The board#

    The app opens on the board with the composer focused: type what the fleet should do and press Enter. Under it, what needs you, then every open order grouped by project (⌘⇧G groups by status). Each card shows its phase, every live agent (role, what each is doing now), its PRs and how far along the train each is. Cards don't move under the pointer or the selection; they resort 2 s after you leave. Machine load and the merge train sit in strips at the bottom.

    When only one order is open it takes the board: the writers still writing, then each PR with its reviewers and verifier under it and how many of its checks are in, and once every PR has merged, what landed.

    The sidebar lists Status, Board, Machines, Time, and each project with its orders; an amber dot means an order needs you. Hold ⌘ to see the ⌘1-⌘9 jump keys. "+ Add" next to Projects adds a project: paste a GitHub URL or owner/repo, or drop a folder or a link on the app. The sheet shows the clone, then what fleetd found and the project's lead (Add a project).

    Give work#

    • Project: the picker (⌘⇧P) says where the order goes and why: chosen, selected in the sidebar, the router's guess, last used, or "the hub picks". A leading @project also picks it.
    • Type: the type picker (⌘⇧T), or a prefix: fix: or bug:, research:, finish:. The default is a feature.
    • Attach: up to 8 files (images, text, PDF; 5 MiB each) by "+ attach" (⌘⇧A), paste or drop. A paste over 3,500 bytes becomes pasted-N.txt. The lead finds them in .fleetd/attachments/<W>/ of the project, kept out of git.
    • Mention: @ opens a menu of the project's files, orders and PRs; ↑ ↓ and Enter or Tab pick one.

    An order is at most 4,000 bytes; attach the detail. It appears as a card at once. If the hub can't tell the project, the card asks which one; if sending fails, it offers Retry and Edit. In an order, ⌘N opens a composer over it, so you keep your place.

    Follow an order#

    Click a card (or select it and press Enter). The left side has its agents, PRs and done-when checks with their results; the right side is the project lead's conversation, focused on this order: finished turns fold to one line ("Worked for 48s · ran 3 commands") and runs about other orders collapse.

    The box under the conversation talks to the lead:

    DoHowThe lead
    Add to the lead's queueEntertakes it in at its next step, after the tool it is running (what that means)
    Interrupt and redirect⌘Enter, or "Interrupt and redirect"stops its current step and reads it at once
    Stop"Stop W…" (⌘⇧.), then confirmits running agents are cancelled and its asks withdrawn; merged PRs stay
    Reorder"Reorder" on a stopped ordernothing yet: its text goes back into the composer, to send as a new order

    Each steer shows its receipt under it: queued, delivered, acknowledged or not acknowledged (Receipts); a queued one has "withdraw". Click an agent's lane to steer that agent instead.

    A stopped order isn't reopened. ⌘[ and ⌘] go to the previous and next order, Esc back to the board.

    Status#

    ⌘. opens Status for what you're looking at: the open order, the selected project, or the whole fleet; ⌘. again goes back. It's also in the sidebar above Board, in the palette ("Status", "Status for W41") and on an order's header. It shows the progress fleetd verified, what the work waits on, what changed since you last looked, then health, what needs you, risks, and the items in progress, next and done. What needs you has the inbox's one-tap answers.

    It updates as things happen, without a refresh. A part whose source has gone quiet gets a warning outline with "stale 4m" and the reason, and keeps its last value. While the app is reconnecting the whole view greys out with "Not live: reconnecting", and after it reconnects it shows empty bars until the fresh numbers arrive. What the number means and what it waits on: Status.

    Inbox and replies#

    ⌘I opens the inbox panel; the badge in the title bar counts what needs you. In the panel, 1-9 select an item, Enter takes its recommendation, n answers no, Esc closes. Or answer a whole batch in one line in its reply field:

    ReplyMeans
    1 goaccept item 1 (also yes, y, ok, rec)
    2 nvmreject item 2 (also no, drop, n)
    3 bpick option b of item 3 (a to f; a is the recommendation)
    4 skipskip a question for now: the lead keeps its recommendation, nothing is recorded
    1-3 gothe same answer for items 1 to 3
    2: use the staging bucketanswer in your own words
    4 laternot now; it stays open
    5 doneyou did a checklist's steps
    recs fineaccept every recommendation not mentioned
    #B4 1 goanswer batch 4, not the latest
    pref: no new dependenciesa standing preference for every lead
    forget D12drop decision 12

    Mix them: 1 go 2 nvm 3 b. A reply the app can't read changes nothing and shows a hint. You have 10 seconds to take back an answer to a question before its lead hears of it. From a terminal, fleetd answer --key-file <human key> lists the open questions with each choice and what it does, and fleetd answer "1 b" --key-file … replies.

    Asks that block work also raise a desktop notification: at most one every 20 minutes, with the rest folded in as "+N more", and only urgent ones in quiet hours (23:00 to 08:00 unless a policy.quiet_hours decision says otherwise). Clicking it opens the ask.

    Settings#

    Settings (⌘,) has General (appearance, motion, the hub connection), Prompts and Instructions; the last two are in What agents are told. Motion: System follows the OS's reduce-motion setting, Reduced turns the brief card and lane transitions off, Full keeps them. Nothing in the app animates continuously.

    Where time went#

    The Time button in an order's header (also ⌘K time, and Time in the sidebar for a project, a machine or the fleet) shows where the time went, in three tabs. Timeline draws each agent, PR, CI check and ask as a lane, with the critical path underlined; hover a bar for the command, its duration and exit code, and click it to open the transcript at that step. Breakdown adds up agent time by group, with the slowest commands and CI checks. Insights lists the findings, each with "show spans" to see the bars behind it. The filter bar picks the range, project, order, machine and role, and compares two periods. How to read it, and what is not exact: Where time went.

    Shortcuts#

    ⌘ on macOS is Ctrl on Linux.

    KeysDoes
    ⌘NNew order (over the order view when one is open)
    ⌘LFocus the composer
    ⌘KCommand palette: projects, orders (by id too), asks and actions; always offers "New order: <what you typed>"
    ⌘IInbox panel
    ⌘0Board
    ⌘1 to ⌘9Open the nth order in the sidebar
    ⌘⇧GGroup the board by project or by status
    ⌘[ / ⌘]Previous / next order
    ⌘.Status for the open order, the selected project or the fleet
    ⌘⇧.Stop the open order (asks to confirm)
    ⌘,Settings
    ⌘BToggle the sidebar
    EnterSend an order; in an order, add to the lead's queue
    ⌘EnterIn an order, interrupt and redirect the lead now
    Shift+EnterNew line
    ⌘⇧P / ⌘⇧T / ⌘⇧AIn the composer: project picker / type picker / attach
    ↑ ↓ (or j k)Move the selection on the board, in a menu or the palette (also Ctrl+P / Ctrl+N in the palette)
    EnterOpen the selected card, run the palette item, pick from a menu
    EscClose a menu, the stop confirm or the inbox panel; from an order, back to the board
    Tab / Shift+TabNext / previous control
    ⌘QQuit
    ⌃⌘F (macOS), F11 (Linux)Full screen
    ⌘H, ⌥⌘H, ⌘M (macOS)Hide, hide others, minimize

    Typing with no field focused goes to the composer on screen. Text fields follow the usual editing keys of each platform (word motion, undo and redo, select all).
