Support

Help with fleetd, fleetd cloud and fleetd’s apps.

Email us

Write to support@fleetd.dev. If your question is about your account, a workspace or billing, send it from your account’s email and say which workspace it is about.

For a problem with the app, tell us what you did, what you saw, and which app you use, on which device.

Service status

See whether fleetd cloud and the website and docs are up at status.fleetd.dev.

Self-hosted fleetd

fleetd is open source. Report a bug or follow its fixes on GitHub, or write to us by email.

Billing and refunds

Manage your plan and payment method in Settings › Workspace › Billing › Manage billing. Refunds are covered by our Refund Policy.
