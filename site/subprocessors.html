Subprocessors

Updated November 28, 2026

The companies that process personal data for fleetd cloud (app.fleetd.dev), what each one gets, and where. Each works only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement with us. How we use them is on Security.

Providers you choose

Some providers get data only because your workspace connects them. They work for you, under your own agreement with them, not for us:

  • Your model provider, such as Anthropic (Claude): agents send it what they work on, with your workspace’s key or a member’s own subscription. See AI models and your content.
  • Your GitHub account and anything else your agents sign in to or connect.

Transfers out of the EU

Where a provider processes data outside the EU, the EEA or the UK, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where it is certified, or on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum.

When this list changes

We tell workspace owners by email, and update this page, at least 30 days before a new provider starts processing your data. If you object on reasonable grounds, tell us at support@fleetd.dev within those 30 days. If we can’t resolve it, you can end your plan and we refund the unused part of what you prepaid.
