fleetd
https://fleetd.sh

Software factories, reinvented as fast as the models improve.

You set the direction. Agents build, review and ship it while you sleep.

  curl -fsSL https://fleetd.sh | sh

macOS or Linux x64. Open source, MIT.
Install:     https://fleetd.sh/docs/install
Quick start: https://fleetd.sh/docs/quick-start
GitHub:      https://github.com/unlaboredlabs/fleetd


SOFTWARE ISN'T AN ASSET. IT'S A DOZEN EXPERIMENTS. KEEP ONE. DROP THE REST.
Agents made building cheap, so stop polishing one bet for months. Ship a dozen
experiments tonight, see what people actually use, and drop the rest without
regret. fleetd is built for that pace: a lead plans the work, agents build it
in parallel, and every change is reviewed before it lands.


ASK THE LEAD; IT LANDS THE PRS
The lead splits your request between agents, each PR gets its own reviewer,
and the merge train lands them together.

YOUR TEAM IN ONE CHAT

AGENTS MESSAGE EACH OTHER, IN THE OPEN
When one agent learns something another needs, it tells it directly. Every
message shows on the agent's work and in the project's log, with who read it
and when.
