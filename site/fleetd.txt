fleetd
https://fleetd.sh

A software factory on your own machines.

You ask a project's lead. Agents build it on your machines, a reviewer checks
every change, and a merge train lands it.

  curl -fsSL https://fleetd.sh | sh

macOS or Linux x64. Open source, MIT.
Install:     https://fleetd.sh/docs/install
Quick start: https://fleetd.sh/docs/quick-start
GitHub:      https://github.com/unlaboredlabs/fleetd


ASK THE LEAD; IT LANDS THE PRS
The lead splits your request between agents, each PR gets its own reviewer,
and the merge train lands them together.
Clip: showcase-lead-dark.webm, showcase-lead-light.webm. Still:
showcase-lead-dark.jpg. More: https://fleetd.sh/docs/merge-train


YOUR TEAM IN ONE CHAT
Everyone on the hub writes in the same project chat, under their own name. The
lead answers all of you.
Clip: showcase-team-dark.webm, showcase-team-light.webm. Still:
showcase-team-dark.jpg. More: https://fleetd.sh/docs/people


WATCH AN AGENT'S SCREEN, AND TAKE OVER
Agents test in a real browser or iPhone simulator while you watch. When one
needs a code or a login, take over, then hand back.
Clip: showcase-screens-dark.webm, showcase-screens-light.webm. Still:
showcase-screens-dark.jpg. More: https://fleetd.sh/docs/browser-sessions


NAVI, YOUR OWN ASSISTANT
A Navi has its own name and chat, and does what you ask. Nothing goes out
until you approve it.
Clip: showcase-navi-dark.webm, showcase-navi-light.webm. Still:
showcase-navi-dark.jpg. More: https://fleetd.sh/docs/navi
