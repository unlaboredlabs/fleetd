OTHER TOOLS GIVE DEVELOPERS MORE THREADS. FLEETD GIVES THE WHOLE TEAM ONE LEAD AGENT.
fleetd puts one lead on each project. It orchestrates thousands of agents,
lands changes autonomously and learns from all requests, retaining all context
of your work.

BRING THE MODELS YOU ALREADY PAY FOR
fleetd sells no tokens. Agents run on your own subscription or API key.
                 Subscription    API key
  Anthropic      Claude          Anthropic API
  Google         Antigravity     Gemini API
  OpenAI         ChatGPT         OpenAI API       (coming soon)
Your agents run on what you connect. A subscription keeps its own usage
limits; a key is billed by its provider. fleetd adds nothing on top.


RUN IT YOURSELF, OR ON FLEETD CLOUD
Self-hosted, free and open source: the workspace and its agents run on your
own machines, with your own Claude and GitHub. Nothing fleetd runs is needed.
fleetd cloud, from $20 a month: fleetd runs the workspace for your team, in the
EU. Agents run on fleetd's computers, or on your own machines at no cost.
More: https://fleetd.sh/pricing


PRICING (FLEETD CLOUD)
fleetd cloud is ready to orchestrate thousands of agents. fleetd is open source
and free to run yourself. On fleetd cloud you pay only for the computers we
run. No seats and no tokens. Free when agents run on your own machines, with
your own Claude login: sign up at https://app.fleetd.dev and connect your
machine. Plans are for agents on our computers, and cover the whole team.

  Free     $0 a month    your own machines run the agents, your own Claude login
  Solo    $20 a month      500 hours of computer time     25 agents at once
  Team    $50 a month    1,000 hours of computer time    100 agents at once
  Scale  $200 a month    4,300 hours of computer time    300 agents at once

Hours are for the smallest computer. Unused time doesn't roll over. Prices are
in US dollars, plus VAT where it applies, shown at checkout. Sold by Unlabored
Labs, Inc. Billing details: https://fleetd.sh/docs/billing

Pay as you go, for extra time beyond a plan or no plan at all. Metered to the
millisecond, only while a computer is running (a month is 730 hours; a second
of the smallest size is $0.0000167):
  2 CPU, 4 GB    $0.06 an hour
  4 CPU, 8 GB    $0.12 an hour
  8 CPU, 16 GB   $0.24 an hour
Credits: prepaid computer time, from $10, valid for 12 months. Without a plan,
credits run up to 25 agents at once.

Estimate a month on https://fleetd.sh/pricing: pick a computer size, how many
agents run at once, the hours each works a day and the days a month. Agents
sleep when they're idle, so a computer is billed only while its agent works.
For example, 25 agents at once on 2 CPU, 4 GB, 3 hours a day for 22 days, is
1,650 hours ($99) of computer time: the Solo plan, $20 a month, runs it, with
$69 past the $30 it includes paid from credits ($89 in all).
