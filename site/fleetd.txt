Software factories, reinvented as fast as the models improve.

You set the direction. Agents build, review and ship it while you sleep.

  curl -fsSL https://fleetd.sh | sh

SOFTWARE ISN'T AN ASSET. IT'S A DOZEN EXPERIMENTS. KEEP ONE. DROP THE REST.
Agents made building cheap, so stop polishing one bet for months. Ship a dozen
experiments tonight, see what people actually use, and drop the rest without
regret. fleetd is built for that pace: a lead plans the work, agents build it
in parallel, and every change is reviewed before it lands.

RUN IT YOURSELF, OR ON FLEETD CLOUD
Self-hosted, free and open source: the hub and its agents run on your own
machines, with your own Claude and GitHub. Nothing fleetd runs is needed.
fleetd cloud, from $20 a month: fleetd runs the hub for your team, in the
EU. Agents run on fleetd's computers, or on your own machines at no cost.
More: https://fleetd.sh/pricing


PRICING (FLEETD CLOUD)
Free on your machines. Pay for ours. A plan pays for fleetd's computers;
your own machines cost nothing, self-hosted or on the cloud.

  Solo    $20 a month    $30 of computer time included    3 agents at once
  Team    $50 a month    $60 of computer time included   10 agents at once
  Scale  $200 a month   $260 of computer time included   40 agents at once

Computer time, charged to the millisecond while a computer runs:
  2 CPU, 4 GB    $0.06 an hour
  4 CPU, 8 GB    $0.12 an hour
  8 CPU, 16 GB   $0.24 an hour

Credits are computer time paid ahead, at face value ($10 buys $10 of time),
from $10, with or without a plan. They last 12 months; a plan's time is used
first and ends with its month. With credits and no plan, 3 agents run at
once. Leads never count toward agents at once. No free tier of fleetd's
computers. Prices are before VAT. Details: https://fleetd.sh/docs/billing
