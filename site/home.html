Other tools give developers more threads. fleetd gives the whole team one lead agent.

fleetd puts one lead on each project. It orchestrates thousands of agents, lands changes autonomously and learns from all requests, retaining all context of your work.

Ask the lead; it lands the PRs

The lead splits your request between agents, each PR gets its own reviewer, and the merge train lands them together.

Merge train

Your team in one chat

Everyone on the hub writes in the same bot’s chat, under their own name. The lead answers all of you.

People

Agents message each other, in the open

When one agent learns something another needs, it tells it directly. Every message shows on the agent’s work and in the bot’s log, with who read it and when.

Agents’ messages

Watch an agent’s screen, and take over

Agents test in a real browser or iPhone simulator while you watch. When one needs a code or a login, take over, then hand back.

Pricing, and how they differ

Self-hosted

Free, open source

The hub and its agents run on your own machines, with your own Claude and GitHub. Nothing fleetd runs is needed.

fleetd cloud

From $20 a month

fleetd runs the hub for your team, in the EU. Agents run on fleetd’s computers, or on your own machines at no cost.
