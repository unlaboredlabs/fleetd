Software isn’t an asset. It’s a dozen experiments. Keep one. Drop the rest.

Agents made building cheap, so stop polishing one bet for months. Ship a dozen experiments tonight, see what people actually use, and drop the rest without regret. fleetd is built for that pace: a lead plans the work, agents build it in parallel, and every change is reviewed before it lands.

Ask the lead; it lands the PRs

The lead splits your request between agents, each PR gets its own reviewer, and the merge train lands them together.

Your team in one chat

Everyone on the hub writes in the same project chat, under their own name. The lead answers all of you.

People

Agents message each other, in the open

When one agent learns something another needs, it tells it directly. Every message shows on the agent’s work and in the project’s log, with who read it and when.
