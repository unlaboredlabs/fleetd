Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

This policy explains what personal data fleetd cloud (app.fleetd.dev), fleetd’s apps and fleetd.sh collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

The controller is Unlabored Labs, Inc., 131 Continental Dr Suite 305, Newark, Delaware 19713, United States, support@fleetd.dev.

If you run fleetd yourself, your data stays on your own machines, and you are its controller. Two things still pass through us: phone notifications and update downloads. A notification is sealed end to end, so our push relay sees only a device token and text it can’t read, and keeps neither. Update downloads come from GitHub.

What we collect

AccountYour email address, your name if you give one, and your sign-in history (time, IP address, device).
WorkspaceWorkspace name, members, roles, invites, bots and their settings, and which GitHub repositories and accounts you connect.

Vault itemsLogins, codes, cards and addresses you save. Vault items are sealed with encryption. They are opened only to fill them in where you or your agents use them, and the apps never show them back.

BillingPlan, credits, invoices, billing address and tax ID. Stripe holds your card. We never see the full number.

Phone appA push token for your device, so notifications reach it.

We don’t collect the content of your accounts at other providers, such as your model provider or GitHub, beyond what your agents use while they work for you.

We don’t sell your data, we don’t advertise, and we don’t use your content to train AI models.

Some other services get data only because you choose to use them:

  • Apple and Google deliver phone notifications. They get your device’s push token and a sealed message they can’t read.
  • Your own providers, such as Anthropic and GitHub, get what your agents send them, under your own agreements with them.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect people’s safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the company. If that happens, we tell you first.

Where your data is

fleetd cloud runs in the EU. Some of our providers are US companies and may process data in the US or elsewhere. Where data leaves the EU, the EEA or the UK, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum).

How long we keep it

  • Account and workspace data for as long as your account or workspace exists.
  • Screen recordings for 30 days by default. A workspace can change this in Settings.
  • Deleted data. When you delete a bot or a workspace, or we delete your account at your request, we delete its data from the live system. Copies in our backups are kept for 30 days and then deleted.
  • Billing records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually up to 10 years.
  • Server logs for 30 days.
  • Support emails for as long as we need them to help you, and at most 2 years after the last message.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • limit how we use it;
  • send it to you or someone else in a common format;
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests.

Write to support@fleetd.dev. We answer within one month. We may need to confirm it’s you first.

You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit.
  • Vault items are sealed with encryption at rest.
  • Each workspace’s data is kept apart from other workspaces’.
  • Only a few people at fleetd can reach production systems, and only when they need to.

If a breach puts your data at risk, we tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

fleetd isn’t meant for anyone under 18, and we don’t knowingly collect children’s data.

Cookies

We don’t use tracking, advertising or analytics cookies, so we don’t show a cookie banner.

  • app.fleetd.dev sets one cookie that keeps you signed in. It also sets a short-lived cookie (10 minutes) while you connect GitHub.
  • fleetd.sh stores only your light or dark theme choice, in your browser’s local storage.
  • Stripe’s checkout and WorkOS’s sign-in pages set their own cookies for security and fraud prevention, under their own policies.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we tell you in advance by email or in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
