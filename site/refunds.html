Refund Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

This policy covers what you buy on fleetd cloud: plans and credits. Stripe handles payments. A refund goes back to the card or account you paid with, usually within 5–10 days.

Within 14 days: your right to withdraw

If you are a consumer in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you can withdraw from any purchase within 14 days of it. You don’t need to give a reason.

You asked us to start at once, so you pay for what you used before you withdrew. We refund the rest:

  • A plan: we refund the plan price minus the computer time you used, at the pay-as-you-go rates, up to the plan price. Your plan ends when we process the refund.
  • Credits: we refund the credits you haven’t used.

To withdraw, write to support@fleetd.dev from your account’s email. Say what you are withdrawing from; the form below is enough. We refund within 14 days of your message.

We offer the same to everyone, consumer or not, for a first purchase made within the last 14 days.

After 14 days

  • Plans: cancel at any time in Settings › Workspace › Billing › Manage billing. Your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for and then stops. We don’t refund part of a month.
  • Credits: we don’t refund credits after 14 days. They stay usable until they expire, 12 months after you bought them. Expired credits aren’t refunded.
  • Computer time already used isn’t refunded.

When we refund anyway

  • A billing error, such as a double charge or a wrong amount: we refund it in full.
  • Our fault: if an outage on our side wasted your computer time, write to us and we give the time back as credits.
  • We close the Service, or end your account without cause: we refund credits you paid for and haven’t used.

We don’t refund an account we close for breaking the Terms’ acceptable use rules, except where the law requires it.

What a refund changes

A refund takes back the credits or included time it paid for, in proportion to the amount refunded. A refunded plan ends.

If you dispute a charge with your bank, the credits from that payment are held until the dispute is settled. Please write to us first; it’s usually faster.
